## 4. Exploratory versus independent

**Everything measured in this run is exploratory.** Vietnam informed the pooling choice,
its REPORT half has been scored in at least four runs, and the hypothesis tested here was
generated by inspecting the full cohort — so neither half is clean for it. Both halves are
reported and agree (primary endpoint, arm L: TUNE 0.6485, REPORT 0.6454 against H's 0.6159
and 0.6189), but that agreement is internal consistency, not independent confirmation.

**TBX11K `internal_test` (901 cases) remains the project's only unexposed partition and
was not read.** RAD-DINO checkpoint exposure to any evaluation cohort is **unknown**.

## 5. Best-supported explanations for the screening gap

**Narrowed to the point of being ruled out:**

- **Scanner / acquisition shift is not the cause of the discrimination gap.** Subspace
  removal was rejected (−0.0002), and stripping 44 of 768 scanner-predictive directions
  left TB AUROC flat while scanner decodability stayed at 0.9118. Real information, not
  load-bearing. It remains an *operating-point* problem.
- **"The model has never seen an abnormal negative chest" is not the cause.** Adding
  TBX11K's 2,700 `sick_non_tb` images (arm S) **hurt slightly**, replicating
  `hard_negatives.json` under a different pooling scheme and a clinically defined endpoint.
- **Prevalence is not the cause.** AUROC is invariant to class balance when the
  class-conditional distributions are fixed.
- **Selection leakage does not explain it.** DA/DB informed no configuration decision and
  still scores 0.9755 / 0.9497.
- **The frozen features are not simply inadequate.** Lunit reaches 0.7827 on identical
  images, so the information is in the pixels.

**Supported, and the clearest single finding:**

> **The gap is concentrated in participants with previous TB.** They are **35.0% of the
> Vietnam negatives**. Discrimination is **0.7811** against negatives without past TB and
> **0.6182** against those with it. The commercial comparator degrades on the same axis
> (0.8170 → 0.7192) but **about 1.7× less**.

**Partially supported:** a **training/evaluation task-definition mismatch**. TBX11K labels
`latent_tb` positive while the Xpert reference calls those participants negative; removing
that contradiction recovers +0.029 of the 0.101 gap to the commercial comparator — real,
mechanistically clear, and **small**.

**Still unidentified:** disease severity (no severity variable in any held cohort),
verification bias in Vietnam (enrolment and testing completeness undocumented), and
whether genuine post-treatment chests in training would help (no such dataset is held).
These cohorts differ on all of these at once, so **no single cause is identifiable from
existing data**.

## 6. Prior art: this is a replication

**VERIFIED, full text read:** Kagujje M, Kerkhoff AD, Nteeni M, Dunn I, Mateyo K, Muyoyeta
M. *Clin Infect Dis* 2022;76(3):e894–e901, DOI 10.1093/cid/ciac679, PMID 36004409. In
1,884 Zambian ACF participants (452 with previous TB, Xpert reference), **CAD4TB
specificity was 24.0% with previous TB against 60.3% without; qXR 22.2% against 61.8%**,
with sensitivity preserved.

**They found it first, in a larger and more clinically rigorous study.** What this run adds
is a refinement, not a discovery: they report *thresholded specificity*, which a per-group
threshold could in principle repair; **this run shows the deficit is present in AUROC, so
it is a ranking failure that recalibration cannot fix.** Plus a research system and a
commercial comparator measured on identical patients.

The honest framing for the write-up: **a replication and benchmarking study with a
preregistered negative-results catalogue**, not a methods paper. The architecture is not
novel (RADAR is closer prior art and adds mitigation this project lacks).

## 7. Highest-value next action

**Obtain a second screening cohort. Specifically, approach the Kagujje / Muyoyeta group at
CIDRZ, Lusaka.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **why them** | they published the past-TB finding, hold a 1,884-participant ACF cohort with 452 previous-TB participants and an Xpert reference, and are already working on this question. Images were not publicly released, so it needs a direct approach |
| **prerequisites** | ethics/IRB cover for secondary AI analysis, a data-sharing agreement, de-identification. Offer to run the analysis inside their environment — the pipeline needs **0.94 GB of VRAM and seven minutes** |
| **cost** | researcher time and 6–18 months of lead time. Negligible compute |
| **expected information gain** | high. It is the only way to answer whether the gap, the past-TB deficit and arm L reproduce outside Vietnam — three questions no held dataset can touch |
| **size needed** | **2,500–4,000 participants** with universal microbiological confirmation, estimating the past-TB subgroup AUROC to ≈±0.035. Budget ~5,000 enrolled for 15–25% attrition. Derived in `05_SAMPLE_SIZE_AND_POWER.md`, not copied |

**Why this beats another architecture sweep.** Every model intervention attempted now has a
measured effect and an interval: pooling +0.028 to +0.072 on curated sets and ≈0 at
screening; subspace removal −0.0002, rejected; scanner offsets +0.0206, CI crossing zero;
extra non-TB negatives −0.014, twice; attention-MIL worse; more training data flat; label
redefinition +0.029, below the meaningful threshold. **Seven interventions, and the
screening number has moved from 0.6151 to 0.7242 — almost all of it from pooling and
masking, with nothing left in the obvious directions.**

Meanwhile the paired SE for comparing two of our own arms is **0.00355**, while the SE for
estimating one arm's actual performance is **0.01912**. We can distinguish our own variants
with 134 subgroup cases and cannot say how good any of them is without about 1,763. **The
project is now precision-limited, not idea-limited.** More sweeps add nothing a new cohort
would not add better.

## 8. Go / no-go

**NO-GO on further model changes before representative data**, with three narrow
exceptions:

1. **Preregister arm L as a primary comparison for the next cohort.** Cheap, mechanistic,
   and the only lead with a consistent measured effect. Do not adopt it now.
2. **Build the scoring pipeline and its tests against synthetic data**, so the new cohort
   is a single command.
3. **Keep `internal_test` unread** until there is a question worth spending it on.

Everything else waits for data.

## 9. What is not claimed

No AUROC improvement was adopted. No clinical readiness. No generalisation beyond the
cohorts measured. No novel architecture. No causal account of the screening gap — only
several candidate causes ruled out and one localised. The past-TB finding is a replication
of published work and must be cited as such.
