## Executive summary

**The label change was rejected on internal data and the rejection is the finding.**
Training the frozen regional-pooling head on TBX11K's documented active-TB target instead
of its any-TB target changes calibration AUROC by **-0.0037** [-0.0081, +0.0002] —
tightly determined, nowhere near the +0.05 declared meaningful, slightly the wrong way.

**But on the Vietnam screening cohort the same change is worth +0.0203** [+0.0127,
+0.0279] (exploratory). Same weights, same change, opposite sign. The internal dataset
cannot see the benefit because separating active from obsolete TB on TBX11K is already
nearly solved (AUROC 0.95, 18 obsolete cases in the evaluation split). Vietnam is where
past TB is 36% of the positives.

**So an internal development split is the wrong instrument for this hypothesis** — and
that is now measured rather than asserted.

**The mechanism was also isolated.** A third arm that *drops* the latent images instead of
relabelling them is worth +0.0023 on Vietnam, against +0.0203 for relabelling — an order
of magnitude apart. The model has to be **shown** that an obsolete-TB radiograph is not
active disease; merely not calling it TB achieves almost nothing. The earlier arm X could
not see this, because it bundled the label change with a negative-composition change.

**One open flag from the previous audit is now closed.** The 901-row sealed result that
looked like it might be a scored pass over `internal_test` **was** the provisional C01
`internal_test` partition — settled from the seal's own metadata, without opening the
split.

## 2. Was the improvement measured on data already exposed to earlier decisions?

**The internal result: no. The external result: yes, heavily.**

- **TBX11K calibration** has never been used by the regional-MIL pipeline — not for
  selection, not for early stopping. It was used once to fit thresholds for the earlier
  global-pooling head, which does not affect AUROC. It is development data from the
  training distribution, so it is internal, but it is not contaminated for this question.
- **Vietnam TUNE selected the pooling configuration.** The +0.0203 is measured on the
  cohort that chose the model it is measured with, for a hypothesis generated by
  inspecting the pooled 2,708 participants. It is labelled exploratory everywhere it
  appears.
- It is also the **third** estimate of substantially the same effect — arm L gave +0.0292
  on Vietnam, the exploratory scanner offset gave +0.0206 on REPORT. Three exploratory
  estimates on exposed data do not add up to one confirmation.

## 3. Did the experiment meet its preregistered criterion?

**No. REJECT**, and the rejection is well powered.

| criterion | required | observed | pass |
|---|---|---|---|
| primary paired delta AUROC | >= +0.05 | -0.0037 | no |
| 95% paired interval excludes zero | yes | [-0.0081, +0.0002] | no |
| interval half-width | <= 0.05 | **0.0041** | yes |
| active-TB sensitivity drop | <= 0.02 | **+0.0123** (rose) | yes |

The half-width is twelve times smaller than the effect the study was built to detect, so
this is not a null from noise. The +0.05 threshold was **retained unchanged** from the
earlier protocol, specifically so it could not be lowered to let the known +0.0292 pass.

## 4. Did sensitivity fall in any relevant subgroup?

**No.** Active-TB sensitivity rose 0.8519 -> 0.8642 at a development-fitted threshold,
while specificity fell 0.9156 -> 0.9071 — an operating-point shift, not a discrimination
gain.

**The guardrail is underpowered and was declared underpowered before fitting.** With 81
positives, a 0.02 change is under two patients.

Sensitivity on `latent_tb` cases necessarily falls under arm B1, because those cases are
negatives by design. That is the intervention, not a harm.

## 5. Where did the change come from — discrimination, calibration, prevalence, target or denominator?

**From the target definition, and it was deliberately prevented from being a denominator
change.** Both arms are scored against a single fixed target (`active_tb_label`) on
identical rows in identical order, declared in the protocol before fitting, precisely
because scoring each arm against its own target would have compared two different
questions. The mirror evaluation against `tb_label` is reported too (-0.0029), and moves
the same way.

Prevalence is constant within each evaluation: 9.01% under `active_tb_label`, 11.01% under
`tb_label`, identical across arms.

The small internal loss decomposes cleanly (post-hoc, labelled post-hoc): the active-vs-
obsolete contrast **improves** 0.7414 -> 0.7524 while the active-vs-non-TB-abnormal
contrast **worsens** 0.9114 -> 0.9035. The intended effect is real and is outweighed by a
class with 22 times the denominator weight.

## 9. Failure cases and defects found

1. **`tb1199`** — the one box-less TB image. `labels_tbx11k_trainval_v1.csv` calls it
   `active_tb`; `provisional_c01.csv` and `p1_make_tbx11k_labels.py`'s documented
   `NO_BOX_FALLBACK` call it `latent_tb`. The published train counts (473/104/23) support
   latent. **The shipped v1 label CSV is wrong on this row.** One image in train; it
   cannot move any figure, and C01 was used.
2. **`active_tb_label` is stored as an empty string**, not `0`, for all 140 latent rows in
   C01. Any consumer reading that column numerically gets silent NaNs. This run derives
   both targets from `category` instead.
3. **The frozen regional feature cache covers only 6,600 of 8,400 images** — train and
   development, i.e. the official TBX11K train list. Calibration and `internal_test` were
   never regionally embedded. This was not documented anywhere and is why the first
   attempt at the primary endpoint aborted.
4. **23 of 899 calibration images have a cross-split near-duplicate** at cosine >= 0.95,
   still unreviewed (P1-07 outstanding). Quantified; the primary endpoint is recomputed
   without them and moves by 0.0003.

## 10. Limitations

- **Exploratory, not confirmatory.** The hypothesis was already in hand and the effect
  already estimated once.
- **Provisional C01.** Every number is conditional on a split that P1 has not finalised.
- **No patient identifiers.** TBX11K ships none. The folds are image-level and are not
  described otherwise anywhere in this run.
- **The external estimate is on an exposed cohort.** Vietnam TUNE selected the pooling
  configuration.
- **TBX11K's own active/obsolete reading is a radiologist reading of a radiograph**, not a
  microbiological result. This project cannot audit it.
- **No causality.** Nothing here shows the classifier uses a visual scarring cue; the
  CLEAR correlations remain associations between a text-similarity proxy and a score.
- **No clinical-readiness or novelty claim.** Previous-TB degradation is already reported
  in the literature (Kagujje 2022, Qin 2024, Codlin 2025).

## 11. The single recommended next step

**Re-run the CLEAR concept audit on Vietnam TUNE over arm B1's scores, in the same job as
a 9x9 concept correlation matrix and a negative-control concept.**

Why this one:

- It is the **only** remaining way to test the mechanism. If relabelling latent TB is
  what produced +0.0203 on Vietnam, the scarring association (rho = 0.566, delta-R2 =
  0.198) should weaken under arm B1. If it does not move at all, then the +0.02 is doing
  something other than what we think, and no concept-informed intervention is warranted.
- The missing ingredient — arm B1's per-case Vietnam TUNE scores — **now exists**
  (`outputs/predictions/vietnam_tune_armB1.csv`). It did not before today.
- The same job settles two open items from the previous audit at no extra cost: whether
  the nine axes are near-collinear (U2) and whether an unrelated concept also tracks the
  score (U4).
- One Kaggle job, TUNE only, aggregates only, no per-case export. Touches nothing
  reserved.

**Explicitly not next:** do not open Vietnam REPORT (the recurrent-TB safety endpoint has
~51 positives and cannot detect the harm it exists to prevent); do not open
`internal_test`; do not add the apical spatial prior or previous-TB-specific calibration;
do not start RayDINO until the licence question has an institutional answer.

**And one thing that is not an experiment:** ask P1/P4 which case list `rehearsal1` scored
(§7), and get the definitive C01 (§10). Both block more than they cost.
